// 01 — about

I build immersive systems in Unity, computer-vision pipelines, and custom haptic hardware — grounded in an HCI background focused on interaction design and structured user evaluation.

I've deployed to Meta Quest, Apple Vision Pro and Valve Index, shipped a consumer AR app to the App Store and Google Play, and contributed to multiple research projects across XR and haptics. My foundation is in C++ and C#, with multi-platform Unity development, real-time rendering optimisation, and low-latency 3D interactive systems.

An MSc in Human-Computer Interaction grounds this work in interaction design and statistical evaluation. I'm particularly drawn to XR applied to the built environment — using real-time visualisation and spatial interaction to change how physical spaces are designed, understood and experienced.

// research · ISMAR 2026

VR Haptics Perception

Two strands of controlled VR studies. Resized bimanual grasping: a single rigid proxy can represent virtual widths from ~0.57× to 2.02× its physical size undetected. Haptic memory: a voice-coil + FSR cube measuring how a haptic illusion decays ~60–70s after visuals are removed.

  • Unity
  • C#
  • ESP32
  • Quest 3

// research · self-directed

Real-Time Computer Vision Pipeline

An end-to-end prototype that recognises handwritten chemical formulas from a live feed (OpenCV pre-processing → PyTorch CRNN with CTC decoding → stability gating) and overlays the balanced reaction in AR when two formulas meet. Shipped as a desktop client and a browser-based phone client over FastAPI.

  • Python
  • OpenCV
  • PyTorch
  • Unity AR
